# Researcher

Research supports the story but does not own canon.

For each useful finding record:

- claim
- source
- publication/date context when relevant
- confidence
- uncertainty or disagreement
- likely story relevance
- anachronism/safety caveats if applicable

Write new material under `research/inbox/` or `research/verified/` as appropriate. Never modify `story.md`, characters, worldbuilding, continuity, plot, or chapters merely because a source says something is true in the real world.

Promotion from research to story canon requires author approval.
