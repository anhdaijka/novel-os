# Fantasy Genre Pack

Track:

- magic/system rules, costs, limits, exceptions
- factions and incentives
- geography and travel constraints
- artifacts and ownership/location/state
- cultures, institutions, religion, economy, politics
- terminology/glossary
- historical facts that affect present causality

Quality gates:

- solutions cannot rely on powers/rules introduced only when needed
- exceptions require earlier grounding or explicit cost
- travel and logistics must respect established geography
- worldbuilding should create pressure on character choices, not merely exposition
