# Romance Genre Pack

Track relationship movement explicitly enough to prevent emotional resets:

- trust
- attraction
- vulnerability
- intimacy
- conflict/barrier
- misunderstanding or information asymmetry
- commitment willingness
- external pressure

Quality gates:

- every major intimacy increase needs a believable cause
- conflict must not depend solely on avoidable communication failure unless that failure is character-grounded
- attraction and compatibility are different dimensions
- setbacks should alter relationship state rather than reset it to chapter one
- the ending must satisfy the selected romance contract (HEA/HFN or intentionally non-genre-romance outcome)
