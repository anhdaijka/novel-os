# Mystery Genre Pack

Use this as an overlay, not a replacement for Story Skills.

Track deliberately:

- central question and subsidiary questions
- clue: introduction, visibility, interpretation, true meaning, payoff
- suspect opportunity/motive/means state
- reader knowledge vs POV knowledge
- red herrings and why they remain fair
- reveal prerequisites
- promises/payoffs

Quality gates:

- the solution must be causally supported before reveal
- no character may act on information they do not possess
- red herrings should arise from truthful evidence, not authorial deception
- final reveal should answer the story's primary promise, not only identify a culprit
