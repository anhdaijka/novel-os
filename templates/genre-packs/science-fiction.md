# Science Fiction Genre Pack

Track:

- technological/scientific assumptions
- capabilities and limits
- resource/energy/logistics constraints
- institutional incentives
- communication/travel latency
- timeline and causality
- what is real research vs fictional extrapolation

Quality gates:

- distinguish known science, plausible extrapolation, and deliberate fictional premise
- technology must create second-order consequences in society and character choices
- do not silently upgrade capabilities to solve a scene
- research notes remain non-canonical until the author chooses the fictional rule
