# Research Pipeline

Research evidence and fictional canon are deliberately separated.

## Lightweight research

For most novels:

```text
browser / book / interview
        ↓
research/inbox
        ↓
verification
        ↓
research/verified
        ↓
author decision
        ↓
canonical world/story file
```

Use `templates/research-evidence.md` for durable notes.

## Zotero activation

Use Zotero when the project becomes source-heavy: historical fiction, hard science fiction, law, medicine, military detail, politics, mythology, or any project where source provenance matters repeatedly.

Zotero remains the **source library**. Novel OS Markdown stores only the research conclusions and references needed by the story workflow. Do not mirror the complete Zotero database into the novel repository.

## Agent research contract

The Researcher must distinguish:

- verified fact
- interpretation
- uncertainty / disagreement
- sensory/detail inspiration
- story proposal

A source-backed real-world fact still does not automatically become fictional canon.

## Promotion gate

Use `prompts/session/research-to-canon.md` to propose moving research conclusions into canonical world/story files. The author approves the exact subset first.
