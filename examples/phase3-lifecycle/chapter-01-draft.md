Mara heard the dead channel because she was late enough to notice silence.

North Station usually crowded her receiver with clipped platform codes, maintenance acknowledgements, and the flat synthetic voice that repeated delays. Between Gate Four and the service stairs, all of it dropped away. A second later a man's voice entered on Channel Zero, a channel the station map marked as sealed.

"Courier Vale. West stairs. Platform nine."

She stopped with one hand on the rail. Nobody used her surname on open maintenance traffic.

The display above her still showed Platform Six.

Mara looked toward the west stairs. The sensible thing was to flag the transmission, hand the receiver to station security, and continue the delivery by the posted route. Instead she waited.

The board flickered.

PLATFORM 6 changed to PLATFORM 9.

Around her, commuters groaned and turned as one body toward the west stairs.

Mara did not move with them. She lifted the receiver closer to her ear.

Channel Zero had gone silent again.

For the first time that morning, being late no longer felt like the problem.
