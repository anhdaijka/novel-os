# Chapter 01 Plan — Signal Zero

**AUTHOR GATE: APPROVED**

## Purpose

Establish Mara's ordinary transit routine, introduce the impossible maintenance signal, and end with a decision to test it rather than dismiss it.

## POV

Mara Vale — third-person limited.

## Starting state

- Mara trusts station systems more than rumors.
- The field receiver is ordinary work equipment.
- No one has reason to believe the sealed channel is active.

## Ending state

- Mara has heard a transmission that matches her next movement.
- She is unsettled but not convinced of its source.
- She decides to listen again on her next delivery leg.

## Scene sequence

1. Routine arrival at North Station.
2. Receiver opens the dead maintenance channel.
3. The transmission names a platform change Mara has not made yet.
4. Mara makes the change and hears the same phrasing repeated.
5. Decision: test the channel rather than report it immediately.

## Promise planted

The signal predicts Mara's route.

## Question opened

Who is transmitting on the dead channel?

Do **not** reveal the abandoned route-simulation beacon in Chapter 01.
