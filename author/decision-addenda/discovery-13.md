# Decision Addendum — Discovery 13

Status: **accepted development decisions**. This addendum exists because `author/decisions.md` is already large; future consolidation may merge these entries back into the main decision log.

### D-047 — Cast follows life topology

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Volume 1 cast deployment follows ordinary social/work topology rather than plot convenience. A character only enters a case when their real role gives them a reason to be there.
- **Implications:** No fixed ghost-hunting party, no required cast check-in every arc, and no character follows Khanh everywhere. Hometown cast recedes when Hanoi work takes over; workplace cast does not invade Case Zero.

### D-048 — Volume 1 deployment phases

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Volume 1 broadly moves through Homecoming → Case Zero → aftermath/family memory → Hanoi return → Case 02 → wider-world expansion → quiet macro-pattern closing.
- **Implications:** Opening centers Khanh/Lan/Hùng with a light Mai appearance; Hạnh enters early but not as a Case Zero investigator. Thủy and Nhân are delayed until after the Case Zero core. Nam/Hà/Đức Anh/Keisuke enter through ordinary work. Duy and Quân are later/outer-edge introductions. Long is not forced into early cases.

### D-049 — Information remains socially distributed

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** No recurring character should hold the full Volume 1 picture. Knowledge stays divided among family memory, market/logistics, ritual experience, work systems, medicine, online folklore and formal procedure.
- **Implications:** Do not create a paranormal strategy meeting that pools every expert into one answer machine. Khanh often connects partial domains rather than owning or controlling them.

### D-050 — Khanh begins his own record only near Volume 1 end

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh does not start as a formal case archivist. Near the end of Volume 1 he begins privately noting strange matters with simple fields such as name, date, source, what seems certain and what remains uncertain.
- **Implications:** The habit should echo grandfather's terse records without becoming chosen-heir symbolism. It marks Khanh's shift from wanting every anomaly closed to accepting responsibility for remembering what remains unresolved.
