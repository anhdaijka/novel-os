# Author Decision Log — Horrible

Decisions below were explicitly accepted during discovery. Future agents must not casually reopen them. Story Skills canon has not yet been initialized.

### D-001 — Project identity

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** `Horrible` is a long-running Northern Vietnam horror / spiritual / ghost novel with a strong mystery component.
- **Reason:** The project is inspired by the author's lived familiarity with spiritual stories and belief, but is fiction rather than memoir.
- **Implications:** Personal experience may inform worldview, emotional truth and texture without requiring literal biographical reproduction.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-002 — Horror-first genre hierarchy

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Horror / spiritual atmosphere is primary; mystery is structural support and must not dominate the novel's overall language.
- **Reason:** The author does not want detective procedural logic to displace ghost, horror and spiritual experience.
- **Implications:** Mystery must sharpen the horror, never sterilize it. Solving mechanism does not necessarily solve haunting.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-003 — Protagonist Khanh

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** The protagonist is Khanh, male, 27, a software engineer who has just returned from several years of onsite work in Japan and will resume work at the Hanoi branch after a few weeks at home.
- **Reason:** Engineering logic creates productive tension with a family environment long exposed to spiritual belief and stories.
- **Implications:** Khanh remains a real working engineer and skeptical believer; no confirmed supernatural ability early.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-004 — Khanh's Japan background

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh experienced several strange or possibly spiritual episodes in Japan. He was never sure whether they were stress, nightmares, projection, coincidence or something local and supernatural. When he called home, his parents tended to reassure or deny rather than deepen his fear.
- **Implications:** Japan is background pressure and may become a later arc, but the novel does not open by making Japan the main supernatural premise.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-005 — Khanh's epistemic flaw and moral arc

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh strongly wants coherent causal explanations. Over time he learns that knowing the truth creates moral responsibility but does not always create a clean obligation to disclose it.
- **Implications:** A future major arc will involve Khanh withholding a terrible truth from an innocent family, then later being forced into sustained contact with one of their descendants or relatives. The secrecy gradually eats at him.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-006 — Khanh's long ethical direction

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh should ultimately continue doing his ordinary work well while becoming more willing to help others and “tích đức” through action rather than cynical dismissal.
- **Implications:** Virtue is not an RPG reward system; good actions do not guarantee supernatural protection or luck.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-007 — Family home and votive-paper business

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh's family runs a long-established votive-paper shop in the family home on a main road directly beside a busy market crossroads. Ritual days, full moons and festival periods can choke the area with customers and traffic.
- **Implications:** The shop is a strong recurring home base and information node, but not the exclusive source of cases.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-008 — Immediate family

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** The family is basically warm and ordinary. Mother primarily runs the shop and is socially perceptive; father is quieter and more practical. Khanh has an older sister, about 7–8 years older, who is a primary-school teacher, married and living in a neighboring district.
- **Implications:** The sister loves Khanh but is practical rather than indulgent. She may think of him when she wants something bought or handled; she does not simply give him everything he wants. Khanh and his brother-in-law are civil but emotionally distant, with no hidden feud required.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-009 — Grandfather

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh's deceased grandfather was widely known in the region for work described variously as divination, ritual, land-reading or “việc âm”. His abilities are not immediately confirmed. He left records, dates, names and notes rather than a magical grimoire.
- **Implications:** Grandfather is a gateway to past memory, not the master explanation of the universe.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-010 — Grandfather's surviving half-brother

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** One younger paternal half-brother of grandfather is still alive. Their father had a second wife and child while away for work. The two branches later acknowledged one another and maintain warm family relations. This half-brother also did some work related to grandfather's spiritual sphere.
- **Implications:** He is a valuable memory witness and alternate perspective, but must not become a living answer database.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-011 — Hometown friend

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh's hometown friend is female, works in logistics near the market crossroads, and belongs to a family that is one of the commune's largest suppliers of vegetables and food.
- **Implications:** Her family's trade naturally gives her broad local social knowledge. She is not default romance and not a permanent Watson sidekick. She and other hometown acquaintances may joke that Khanh is “Việt kiều Nhật”.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-012 — Case resolution taxonomy

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Cases may be solved, mostly solved, partially solved, unresolved, dormant, intentionally ambiguous or wrongly closed.
- **Implications:** Khanh is not more omnipotent than professional investigators. Unresolved cases must still be intentionally designed rather than left vague from lack of author knowledge.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-013 — Supernatural epistemology

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** The dead are not automatically benevolent, evil, truthful or omniscient. Oan hồn and dying people may hide or manipulate information because of shame, revenge, self-interest or unresolved goals. Possession-like or corpse-reanimation folklore may appear but should remain mysterious and potentially interpretable rather than establishing a rigid non-human taxonomy.
- **Implications:** Strong supernatural experiences can occur without clean cosmological confirmation.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-014 — Case Zero

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Case Zero is “the order for someone not yet dead”. A woman orders highly specific votive items for a living man; he dies four days later. Khanh initially only tries to identify the customer. The present death is plausibly an accident, while a buried family secret from about thirty years earlier and an unidentified woman on CCTV produce a human explanation plus unresolved supernatural residue.
- **Implications:** Case Zero is approximately 12–16 chapters, largely resolved at human level but not metaphysically closed. Grandfather's notebook only appears near the end with the dead woman's name and a terse note such as “Không nhận lễ.”
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-015 — Case Zero ambiguity

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Keep at least three viable purchaser models: human impersonation; supernatural appearance of the woman dead for thirty years; or psychological / identity explanation involving the surviving sister. No model is forced to win at this stage.
- **Implications:** Evidence must remain compatible with the accepted ambiguity and cannot later be casually retconned.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-016 — Work life

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** After the break Khanh returns to Hanoi as a senior-ish full-stack engineer at a Vietnam–Japan technology company. He remains hands-on and can communicate directly with the Japanese side; he is considering technical track versus BrSE / lead growth.
- **Implications:** Work is a genuine narrative engine, not filler. Khanh lives alone in a normal apartment and commutes mainly by motorbike. The apartment is not default haunted-space material.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-017 — Workplace cast

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Workplace includes a competent pragmatic male team lead around 35; a smart, fast, online and slightly chaotic male junior engineer around 23–24; and a female BA/BrSE around Khanh's age who is organized and socially perceptive.
- **Implications:** No early default romance. A Japanese-side recurring counterpart exists but is not immediately tied to supernatural Japan backstory.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-018 — Case 02

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Case 02 candidate is “Tài khoản của người chết”: a deceased engineer's credential appears in a production incident. Human misuse explains most of the security mystery, while a later system activity remains difficult to reproduce or attribute.
- **Implications:** Case 02 has no direct grandfather / Case Zero conspiracy connection. It shifts the uncanny from village-memory systems to digital identity and institutional traces.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-019 — Long-form rhythm

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Cases are separated by real life. There may be days, weeks or months between them. Supporting characters continue their jobs, families and problems when no case is active.
- **Implications:** No mandatory ghost-hunting team and no relentless case-of-the-week cadence.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-020 — Volume architecture

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Organize the long series into Volumes rather than one undifferentiated run. A working scale is roughly 35–55 chapters per Volume, with short, medium and major cases of different lengths.
- **Implications:** Chapter and case counts are rhythm guides, not quotas. Ordinary-life chapters and uncanny incidents that never become cases are valid.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-021 — Macro mystery is a pattern, not a final boss

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** A long-range macro mystery exists around present-day phenomena that sometimes echo grandfather's old records, but only a minority of cases need genuine connection.
- **Implications:** Independent cases remain important. False connections and Khanh overfitting patterns are allowed. Do not collapse the whole series into one ancient demon, conspiracy or supernatural organization.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-022 — Preferred macro direction

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** The preferred macro direction is a hybrid of grandfather's recorded refusals (`Không nhận lễ`, `Không xem`, `Không can thiệp`) and a thematic pattern of truths that the living tried to bury but which leave returning traces.
- **Implications:** Grandfather knew more than Khanh but did not know everything. Different refusals may have different reasons. Identity uncertainty — “who is actually speaking?” — becomes a later escalation rather than an early cosmological answer.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-023 — Depth escalation, not power escalation

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Escalation should move from isolated anomalies toward patterns, conflicting testimony, historical repetition, identity uncertainty and uncertainty about the source of communication.
- **Implications:** Do not default to ma → quỷ → đại quỷ → thần, bloodline powers, magical levels or supernatural combat. Strongly supernatural cases remain rare and exceptional.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-024 — Japan returns through ordinary life

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Japan may return as a substantial arc only after the Vietnam-side identity is established, tentatively Volume 3 or later, through a plausible work reason such as business travel or a short onsite assignment.
- **Implications:** Japanese strange experiences do not need to share one entity or conspiracy with Vietnamese cases. Cultural interpretation of ambiguous contact may become the deeper connection.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-025 — Do not confirm Khanh as a supernatural magnet

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** The series must preserve ordinary explanations for why Khanh encounters more strange stories: attention, family network, work, social word-of-mouth and selection bias.
- **Implications:** Khanh may ask “why me?” without the narrative confirming chosen-one logic. Slow word-of-mouth can cause people to seek him out, but he does not charge money or turn it into a profession.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-026 — Provisional Volume themes

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Volume 1 works under `Những chuyện tưởng đã kết thúc`; Volume 2 under `Người kể không phải lúc nào cũng đáng tin`; Volume 3 under `Ai đang nói?`.
- **Implications:** Volume 1 emphasizes traces and what the dead leave; Volume 2 tests testimony and eventually causes Khanh to trust a wrong message/interpretation with meaningful consequences; Volume 3 deepens identity horror and may include the work-driven Japan return. Later Volumes stay unlocked.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-027 — Khanh's three long arcs

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh develops across three linked axes: answer-seeking → responsibility for knowledge; cynical avoidance of others' burdens → service without guaranteed reward; need for closure → ability to live honestly with uncertainty.
- **Implications:** He remains a software engineer. Cases may strain career and life but do not automatically turn him into a spiritual professional.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-028 — Macro mystery may remain metaphysically incomplete

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** The series may eventually explain what grandfather knew, why he refused certain matters and how some patterns recur without fully explaining what happens after death.
- **Implications:** Readers may debate individual events, but the cumulative series should not collapse into “everything might have been hallucination.” Something beyond current human explanation should remain strongly plausible.
- **Supersedes:** none
