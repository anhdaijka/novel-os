## Core creative contract

- Horror / spiritual experience first; mystery second.
- Mystery must sharpen the horror, never sterilize it.
- Solving mechanism does not necessarily solve haunting.
- Everyday Vietnamese life remains active between cases.
- Culture is not supernatural evidence.
- Folk belief / testimony / character interpretation / observed phenomenon / author-canon supernatural rule remain separate layers.
- The dead are not automatically truthful, omniscient, benevolent or evil.
- Genuine supernatural reality stays strongly plausible at series level while cosmology remains incomplete.
- No default chosen-one, bloodline power, demon-boss or supernatural-combat escalation.
- Khanh remains a working software engineer.

## Main protagonist

**Nguyễn Minh Khanh**, 27, software engineer recently returned from several years onsite in Japan. He is a skeptical believer and causal/root-cause thinker with no confirmed supernatural ability.

His long arcs:
1. wanting answers → accepting responsibility for what he knows;
2. avoiding other people's burdens → helping when he genuinely can without guaranteed reward;
3. demanding closure → living honestly with uncertainty.

Near the end of Volume 1, not before, Khanh begins a simple private record of strange matters: name, date, source, what seems certain, what remains uncertain. It should echo grandfather's notebooks without creating chosen-heir symbolism.

## Setting

- Fictional semi-urbanizing hometown on the former Bắc Giang side of the contemporary Bắc Ninh region, roughly 1–2 hours from Hanoi.
- Family house/votive-paper shop sits beside a busy market crossroads with pre-dawn food/logistics activity.
- Case Zero opens late winter / early spring around Rằm tháng Giêng.
- Kinh Bắc cultural memory coexists with industry, logistics, roads, remaining fields, waterways, cemeteries and ordinary modern life.
- Real Buddhism, Đạo Mẫu / Thờ Mẫu, ancestor practice and folk ritual are living traditions, not interchangeable occult systems.

## Narrative voice

- Close third-person limited around Khanh; one consciousness per scene.
- Contemporary Vietnamese with neutral-to-Northern register and natural present-tense-like flow.
- Medium prose density; lyrical only when earned.
- `Describe the wrongness before naming the horror.`
- `Concrete first, interpretation second.`
- `Fear lives in interruption of normal patterns.`
- `The dead should feel like former people before supernatural objects.`
- Avoid generic AI abstraction, imported gothic language, repetitive cliffhangers and dry procedural rhythm.

## Folklore / embedded-story engine

Stories told by other people are a major independent horror channel. They may be exaggerated, mistaken, contradictory or genuinely supernatural and do not automatically bind main-universe rules.

Core rules:
- Folklore Atlas tracks provenance/context, not monster taxonomy.
- Modern viral folklore does not become historical tradition without sourcing.
- Not every ghost story needs Khanh, investigation or payoff.
- If an oral-story claim crosses into the main plot, re-evaluate it through evidence and author-status rules.
- Absence of established supernatural ability is not permission to invent ability.

## Cast deployment — Volume 1

Core law: **cast follows life topology, not plot convenience**.

### Homecoming

Open small: Khanh, Lan, Hùng, then a light Mai appearance. Hạnh enters early through ordinary family life. The Case Zero customer should arrive around Chapter 2–3 rather than dominate Chapter 1.

### Case Zero

Primary social field: Khanh / Lan / Mai / Hùng / Hạnh.

- Lan provides shop/social memory.
- Mai helps only where local/logistics knowledge gives her reason; she recedes as the matter becomes painful family history.
- Hùng and Hạnh provide practical/moral boundaries.
- Thủy and Nhân do **not** solve the case.
- Duy, Quân, Long and workplace cast do not become Case Zero resources.

### Aftermath / family memory

Grandfather notebook enters near the end. Thủy can be introduced through ordinary cultural/commercial life rather than supernatural diagnosis. Nhân appears only after the main Case Zero resolution through normal family topology; he may recognize refusal-note conventions but does not decode them.

### Hanoi return

Use roughly three chapter-equivalents of actual life transition:
1. departure/aftermath;
2. Hanoi/apartment/office reset;
3. full work-life chapter with only a small dead-account anomaly near the end.

### Case 02 — `Tài khoản của người chết`

Workplace cast dominates.

- Đức Anh preferably notices the dead username first.
- Khanh initially reads it as credential/process failure.
- Nam insists on proper scope/reporting and later pushes back on Khanh digging beyond team responsibility.
- Hà contributes project/client/source context but is not a ghost investigator.
- Keisuke appears lightly through normal work channels.
- Hometown cast recedes naturally.

### Wider world

Duy may enter later through medically plausible circumstances. Quân should remain outer-edge until late Volume 1, preferably first encountered through an exaggerated online retelling of a story Khanh already heard. Long is not forced into either early case.

## Accepted cases

### Case Zero — `Đơn hàng cho người chưa chết`

Working length 12–16 chapters. A highly specific votive order is placed for a living man; he dies four days later. Human/family history explains much of the pressure and the death remains plausibly accidental, while purchaser identity remains unresolved under multiple viable models. Grandfather notebook appears only near the end with the dead woman's name and a refusal note such as `Không nhận lễ`.

### Case 02 — `Tài khoản của người chết`

Modern Hanoi/work case. A dead engineer's credential appears in a production incident. Human misuse explains most of the technical mystery; a later system residue remains difficult to attribute. No direct Case Zero/grandfather conspiracy link.

## Volume 1 macro direction

Working theme: `Những chuyện tưởng đã kết thúc`.

Broad movement:
Homecoming → Case Zero → aftermath/family memory → Hanoi return → Case 02 → wider-world texture → quiet recognition that grandfather's refusal notes recur across apparently unrelated matters.

The Volume ending should reveal a **pattern**, not a final explanation, entity or conspiracy.
