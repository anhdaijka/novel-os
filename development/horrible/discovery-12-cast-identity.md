# Discovery 12 — Cast Naming & Character Identity Pass

Status: **accepted development material, not yet Story Skills canon**.

This pass converts recurring roles into concrete people with names, ages, ordinary lives, contradictions, epistemic styles and storytelling styles. These identities are accepted for development; Story Skills character files should still be initialized selectively after the remaining pre-draft checks.

## Character identity template

Every recurring character should eventually track:

1. `public_role` — how others know them.
2. `private_want` — what they actually want.
3. `contradiction` — two true traits in tension.
4. `pressure_response` — how they react under stress.
5. `epistemic_style` — how they decide what counts as true.
6. `spiritual_posture` — specific posture toward spiritual claims, beyond believer/skeptic labels.
7. `storytelling_style` — how they tell, edit or refuse ghost stories.
8. `life_outside_khanh` — work, family and obligations that continue without the protagonist.

## Nguyễn Minh Khanh — protagonist

- Age: 27.
- Profession: software engineer / senior-ish full-stack engineer at a Vietnam–Japan technology company.
- Approximate appearance: 1.74–1.76 m, moderately slim, short hair, usually slightly tired around the eyes; neat practical clothing without fashion-conscious protagonist styling.
- Recently returned after several years of onsite work in Japan.
- Habit: rereads messages/logs, screenshots or notes things that do not fit a pattern.
- Ordinary flaw: can be organized about important systems while procrastinating trivial personal tasks such as replacing household items or making routine calls.
- Epistemic style: causal coherence, contradiction, reproducibility and root-cause logic.
- Pressure response: freeze/withdraw/quiet analysis more readily than dramatic panic; childhood fear can still appear physically before his reasoning catches up.
- Storytelling style: bad at making ghost stories scary because he keeps qualifying and analyzing them.
- Long contradiction: skeptical believer who genuinely wants evidence but is increasingly unable to dismiss what he experiences.
- Family normally calls him `Khanh`; no fixed childhood nickname is required.

## Nguyễn Văn Hùng — father

- Age: about 57.
- Background: practical construction/technical work; may now take small building, repair, material or related jobs while helping family as needed.
- Personality: quiet, action-first, dislikes making a problem larger through talk.
- Epistemic style: trusts practical experience, physical reality and what can actually be done.
- Spiritual posture: does not deny the spiritual world, but avoids discussing it more than necessary because he grew up close to grandfather's work and saw both successes and failures.
- Contradiction: knows more than he says; treats silence as protection even when silence may create distance.
- Storytelling style: extremely compressed. If he ever tells a strange story in detail, that detail should matter.
- Relationship with grandfather: respect mixed with firsthand knowledge of grandfather's limitations.

## Trần Thị Lan — mother

- Age: about 55.
- Role: primary operator of the family votive-paper shop.
- Personality: fast, practical, commercially experienced, excellent at remembering faces and customer behavior.
- Epistemic style: social observation and decades of repeated human patterns.
- Spiritual posture: pragmatic believer who has heard too many stories to laugh everything off, but does not assume every customer story is true.
- Contradiction: often reads people correctly, which can make her overconfident in intuition.
- Storytelling style: one of the strongest oral-horror voices in the series. Starts with ordinary commercial detail before the uncanny arrives.
- Voice anchor: `Người ta kể thế, còn thật đến đâu ai biết.` Use naturally rather than as a catchphrase in every appearance.

## Nguyễn Minh Hạnh — older sister

- Age: about 35.
- Profession: primary-school teacher.
- Relationship with Khanh: practical affection; she often thinks of him when she wants something bought, ordered, fixed or handled rather than constantly providing things for him.
- Personality: organized, direct, capable of managing people and small crises.
- Epistemic style: tries to separate what children/adults actually observed from what adults taught them to fear.
- Spiritual posture: `Có thì có, nhưng đừng tự dọa mình.`
- Contradiction: sensible skepticism can make her dismiss a strange detail because she assumes adults created the fear first.
- Story ecosystem: school/children/parent folklore can enter through her without turning the school into a permanent haunted setting.

## Lê Đức Tuấn — brother-in-law

- Age: about 37.
- Work: technical / electrical-water / operations-type local work; exact employer can be set later.
- Relationship with Khanh: civil, no conflict, no grudge, simply difficult to become close because both men are not naturally conversational together.
- Spiritual posture: does not deny, but prefers referring serious spiritual matters to someone who actually practices rather than discussing them himself.
- Rule: do not retroactively give the awkwardness a hidden betrayal or supernatural cause without explicit author approval.

## Hoàng Ngọc Mai — hometown logistics friend

- Age: 27.
- Background: grew up near Khanh and the market; the two families have long familiarity.
- Work: logistics/order/driver/customer coordination inside her family's large vegetable and food wholesale/supply business; functionally an operations manager even if the family business uses no formal title.
- Personality: quick, practical, confident, mildly cynical, observant, teasing without becoming a `cool-girl` trope.
- Epistemic style: operational reality — routes, times, vehicles, people, deliveries, warehouses and what actually happened on the ground.
- Spiritual posture: practical half-believer. Accepts that some things are difficult to explain but dislikes superstition being used to excuse carelessness.
- Contradiction: efficient problem-solving can make her force an issue into a practical explanation too early.
- Relationship with Khanh: comfortable long familiarity; no deliberate early romance framing.
- Story ecosystem: drivers, market workers, suppliers, warehouses, night deliveries and roadside stories.
- Storytelling style: mocks a story first, then becomes quieter when the detail she cannot dismiss arrives.
- `Việt kiều Nhật` remains an occasional hometown joke, not a constant nickname.

## Nguyễn Văn Nhân — grandfather's younger paternal half-brother

- Age: mid-70s, approximately 73–77.
- Kinship: younger half-brother of Khanh's grandfather, same father and different mother; the branches now maintain warm relations.
- Work/history: performed some related spiritual/ritual/divination-type work but was less publicly renowned than Khanh's grandfather.
- Personality: more conversational and socially easy than the remembered grandfather; some subjects make him abruptly cautious.
- Epistemic style: experience plus explicit respect for uncertainty.
- Typical language: `Người ta gọi thế`, `Ở chỗ ông thì họ bảo...`, `Ông cũng không dám chắc.`
- Important contradiction: he has expertise but does not agree with everything Khanh's grandfather did.
- Macro role: knows some events and interpretations, never the complete pattern and never becomes a living answer database.
- Exact kinship address Khanh uses for him must be checked against family relationship conventions before final prose.

## Bùi Thị Thủy — recurring female practitioner

- Age: about 49.
- Development label: practitioner associated with Thờ Mẫu / ritual life; exact formal title and practice should be research-confirmed before Story Skills canonization.
- Ordinary work: runs a small fabric/clothing shop.
- Personality: lively, direct, ordinary outside ritual contexts; can move from ritual discussion to electricity bills or shop problems without changing into a mystical NPC.
- Epistemic style: ritual tradition, long experience, social reading and spiritual interpretation.
- Spiritual posture: committed practitioner, but her interpretation is still testimony rather than author voice.
- Strength: understands ritual/social context well.
- Blind spot: can interpret ambiguous phenomena through her own worldview too quickly.
- Relationship with Khanh: thinks he is stubborn but respectful and sometimes teases him.
- Storytelling style: matter-of-fact; frightening things are often told with little theatricality.
- Moral-question line available for the right later moment: `Biết hết rồi thì mày định làm gì với cái biết ấy?` Do not overuse or force it into an early quote-card moment.

## Phạm Quốc Duy — skeptical professional

- Age: about 31–33.
- Profession: emergency physician rather than a forensic specialist, avoiding implausibly convenient investigative access.
- Personality: calm, evidence-oriented, dry rather than combative.
- Epistemic style: threshold of evidence and differential explanation.
- Spiritual posture: does not need to declare metaphysical beliefs; working stance is `Tôi chưa thấy lý do phải dùng giả thuyết đó.`
- Storytelling style: describes phenomenon and stops before interpretation.
- Function: strong skeptic who can remain a skeptic without being written as stupid, arrogant or destined for conversion.

## Đỗ Minh Quân — younger believer / paranormal content creator

- Age: about 24.
- Work: TikTok/YouTube/Facebook-style supernatural/strange-story content; abandoned places, audience submissions, local mysteries and urban legends.
- Spiritual posture: genuinely believes rather than cynically scamming.
- Strengths: curious, willing to approach witnesses, fast at finding obscure story leads.
- Weakness: platform incentives make him overstate certainty, package ambiguity into strong titles/thumbnails and merge versions too quickly.
- Epistemic style: testimony + pattern-recognition with a threshold that is too low.
- Storytelling style: polished for an audience; every Quân retelling should be treated as edited media, not raw testimony.
- Relationship with Khanh: methodological friction; Khanh dislikes his certainty but cannot dismiss that Quân sometimes finds real leads.

## Trần Hoàng Nam — Engineering Team Lead

- Age: about 35.
- Role: Engineering Team Lead.
- Personality: competent, pragmatic, dry humor, not toxic and not a micromanager.
- Relationship with Khanh: respects Khanh's technical ability, notices his tendency to chase root cause beyond current scope.
- Epistemic style: actionable engineering evidence and delivery risk.
- Blind spot: may underestimate human context when delivery pressure is high.
- Spiritual posture: mostly uninterested unless a claim affects a real project/person.
- Humor example available sparingly: `Ma mà fix được memory leak thì anh cấp account.`

## Vũ Đức Anh — junior engineer

- Age: 24.
- Role: junior/younger engineer on or near Khanh's team.
- Personality: fast, enthusiastic, highly online, experimental.
- Strength: searches quickly, uses tools fearlessly, finds scattered digital information.
- Weakness: overfits patterns and treats suggestive evidence as stronger than it is.
- Spiritual posture: half-joke, half-belief; consumes paranormal/urban-legend media.
- Story ecosystem: social media, Reddit-like forums, Facebook groups, TikTok and digital folklore.
- Evidence quality from his story leads is often lower than Lan's long local memory or Nhân's firsthand experience.

## Lê Thu Hà — BA / BrSE

- Role: BA/BrSE with strong Japanese communication and product/client coordination.
- Personality: organized, socially perceptive, composed.
- Relationship with Khanh: she often sees the difference between technical truth and the decision a client/user actually needs.

## Cast-wide epistemic contrast

The recurring ensemble should not reduce spiritual discussion to `believer versus skeptic`.

- Khanh — causal coherence / root cause.
- Hùng — practical experience and action.
- Lan — long social memory and people-reading.
- Hạnh — child/adult behavior and ordinary explanation.
- Mai — operational reality and local logistics.
- Nhân — experiential tradition plus uncertainty.
- Thủy — ritual interpretation and lived practice.
- Duy — evidence threshold / differential explanation.
- Quân — testimony and fast pattern recognition, often too confidently.
- Nam — actionable engineering evidence and scope.
- Đức Anh — digital search and online pattern culture.
- Thu Hà — source qualification and stakeholder context.
- Keisuke — professional precision, spiritual position initially irrelevant.
- Long — procedural/admissible evidence.

This creates conflict through **different standards of knowing**, not repetitive dialogue in which one person says `ma có thật` and another says `ma không tồn tại`.

## Cast-wide storytelling ecosystem

Different recurring characters supply different kinds of oral horror:

- Lan: customer/shop/family stories, ordinary-detail-first.
- Mai: driver, market, warehouse, road and night-logistics stories.
- Hạnh: schoolchildren, parents and school-community stories.
- Nhân: old local belief, practitioner memories and competing interpretations.
- Thủy: ritual/practitioner experiences, told matter-of-factly.
- Duy: clinically observed oddities with no supernatural conclusion.
- Quân: edited digital folklore and audience stories.
- Đức Anh: low-threshold internet urban legends.
- Thu Hà: carefully source-qualified family/social anecdotes.

No character is a universal lore database.

## Family naming / realism rule

The paternal family uses surname `Nguyễn` in the accepted development identities. Other recurring characters may share common Vietnamese surnames; do not artificially avoid duplicate surnames merely to simplify reader tracking. Relationship and given-name context should do the work.
