## Current identity

`Horrible` is horror/spiritual fiction with disciplined mystery structure, not detective fiction with ghosts attached.

Core rules:

- Mystery must sharpen the horror, never sterilize it.
- Solving the mechanism does not necessarily solve the haunting.
- Culture is not supernatural evidence.
- Folklore is evidence of what people believe, not automatic species/cosmology canon.
- A story told inside the novel may be more supernatural than the main narrative can prove.
- Absence of established supernatural ability is not permission to invent ability.
- Cast follows life topology, not plot convenience.
- Information remains socially distributed.

Workflow rules from Discovery 15:

- **Plan until the next irreversible mistake is prevented. Draft before planning starts replacing discovery.**
- **Prose is also research.**

The Volume contains **two full mysteries plus one non-investigative spiritual haunting**. Horror/spiritual experience must remain meaningful when no culprit or mechanism needs solving.

## Short Haunting 03 — `Người về ăn giỗ`

Accepted development truth:

- an older woman died roughly 6–8 years earlier through an ordinary death, not an unresolved murder;
- she left an unfulfilled request to return a small box of photographs/letters/personal material to an estranged relative;
- Khanh enters the household through ordinary shop/family logistics during a death-anniversary gathering;
- family members disagree mildly over whether recurring anniversary phenomena mean she returns;
- Khanh briefly sees an ordinary-looking older woman walk through the busy house and initially assumes she is alive;
- his later description contains one high-value embodied habit/detail associated with the deceased that he had no obvious reason to know;
- there is no useful camera evidence and no practitioner solution;
- Khanh deliberately refuses to convert the event into a formal investigation;
- the family eventually fulfills the promised human action because it is worthwhile whether or not a ghost caused anything;
- no `siêu thoát` or causal supernatural confirmation follows;
- a later grandfather-note overlap may use the same locality/family name plus `Không xem`, but must remain ambiguous.

Default author status: **strongly implied supernatural / deliberately not mechanized**.

## Volume 1 ending knowledge boundary

Reader can know with high confidence:

- Case Zero's human layer is mostly understood but the purchaser remains unresolved.
- Case 02 contains real human credential misuse plus a later unexplained technical residue.
- Khanh has experienced at least one direct event he personally finds very difficult to naturalize.
- Grandfather was fallible and sometimes refused, failed or did not know.
- Grandfather's notebooks contain repeated refusal language across apparently unrelated matters.
- Case 02 is not in grandfather's records.
- Khanh begins a private evidence-oriented note system.

Reader should **not** yet know:

- whether one entity/cause connects the cases;
- why grandfather refused specific matters;
- how communication from the dead works;
- whether Khanh is unusually sensitive;
- whether unusual stories objectively cluster around him;
- the final afterlife cosmology or a bestiary.

The Volume-ending reveal is a **pattern of refusal, not its cause**.

Khanh's private notes use fields such as name/title, date, source, what seems certain and what remains uncertain. This parallels grandfather's notebooks without chosen-heir or paranormal-career framing.
